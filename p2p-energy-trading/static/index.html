    Decentralized microgrid energy trading, signed and sealed.

    A peer-to-peer trading engine where every energy voucher is signed with RSA, encrypted with ElGamal, checked against the meter and agreed by three ledger nodes. Everything on this page runs on a Python backend.

      Three-node ledger

      Each node keeps its own balances and hash-linked chain. A voucher settles when 2 of 3 nodes accept it. Mark a node faulty and it votes the opposite way.
